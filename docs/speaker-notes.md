# Speaker Notes for Presentation

## Slide 1

To begin, let's establish the context for this work, starting with the broader field of Medical Visual Question Answering.

Medical VQA is an advanced adaptation of general VQA. Its primary purpose is to support clinical diagnosis. It is a multimodal task that combines sophisticated image analysis with natural language processing. This allows the system to provide reasoned answers to complex medical questions. As the diagram illustrates, the model takes various inputs. These can include medical images like X-rays, CT-scans or MRIs, along with textual information from an Electronic Health Records. The AI model then processes this combined information to generate an answer. This answer ultimately assists in clinical decision-making and has the potential to help reduce diagnostic errors.

My thesis focuses on a more specialized and clinically nuanced sub-task. We are investigating Medical Difference VQA. This field is specifically designed to reason about the differences between longitudinal medical images. Instead of analyzing a single scan, the model compares a patient's current image with a previous one. This process directly mirrors how a radiologist tracks disease progression or evaluates treatment response over time, making it a critical area for research.
